A project here is a budget of hours — how much work it is in total, and how many hours you usually put in per day. The planner works out the finish date and keeps it honest as your days change.

You enter a project as a budget of hours — how much work it is in total and how many hours you usually get done in a day. The finish date is worked out for you and shifts as you adjust individual days. In Move projects mode, drag a bar to reschedule it, or click any day to open it in detail. In Paint days mode, pick a value and drag across the days it should apply to — press Esc when you’re done. The bottom band adds every project together: columns are hours, and the colored strip underneath shows how full each day is. Anything can be reverted with Undo.
